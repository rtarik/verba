These verbs are **not irregular**. They are pronounced exactly as the regular pattern demands —
it is the *spelling* that has to bend, because Spanish writes the same sound with different
letters depending on the vowel that follows.

The two facts behind almost everything on this page:

| letter | before **a, o, u** | before **e, i** |
|---|---|---|
| c | hard *k* — `casa`, `coche` | soft *th/s* — `cena`, `cine` |
| g | hard *g* — `gato`, `gota` | soft *h* — `gente`, `gimnasio` |

So when an ending starting with `e` arrives after a `c`, the `c` must become `qu` to keep sounding
like a *k*. Nothing about the verb has changed; only the spelling has moved to keep up.

## -car, -gar, -zar

These change **wherever an `e` follows** — which means the `yo` form of the preterite and the
whole present subjunctive, and therefore most of the imperative.

| | buscar (-car) | llegar (-gar) | empezar (-zar) |
|---|---|---|---|
| presente | busco, buscas, busca, buscamos, buscáis, buscan | llego, llegas, llega, llegamos, llegáis, llegan | empiezo, empiezas, empieza, empezamos, empezáis, empiezan |
| pretérito | bus**qu**é, buscaste, buscó, buscamos, buscasteis, buscaron | lle**gu**é, llegaste, llegó, llegamos, llegasteis, llegaron | empe**c**é, empezaste, empezó, empezamos, empezasteis, empezaron |
| pres. subj. | bus**qu**e, bus**qu**es, bus**qu**e, bus**qu**emos, bus**qu**éis, bus**qu**en | lle**gu**e, lle**gu**es, lle**gu**e, lle**gu**emos, lle**gu**éis, lle**gu**en | empie**c**e, empie**c**es, empie**c**e, empe**c**emos, empe**c**éis, empie**c**en |

### -car verbs (c → qu)

`buscar` (busqué), `tocar` (toqué), `sacar` (saqué), `explicar` (expliqué), `practicar`
(practiqué), `aparcar` (aparqué), `acercarse` (me acerqué), `arrancar` (arranqué), `criticar`
(critiqué), `dedicar` (dediqué), `equivocarse` (me equivoqué), `indicar` (indiqué), `marcar`
(marqué), `pescar` (pesqué), `secar` (sequé), `significar` (signifiqué).

### -gar verbs (g → gu)

`llegar` (llegué), `pagar` (pagué), `jugar` (jugué), `apagar` (apagué), `entregar` (entregué),
`colgar` (colgué), `castigar` (castigué), `investigar` (investigué), `negar` (negué), `obligar`
(obligué), `pegar` (pegué), `rogar` (rogué), `tragar` (tragué), `cargar` (cargué), `encargar`
(encargué).

### -zar verbs (z → c)

`empezar` (empecé), `almorzar` (almorcé), `cruzar` (crucé), `comenzar` (comencé), `alcanzar`
(alcancé), `organizar` (organicé), `realizar` (realicé), `utilizar` (utilicé), `abrazar`
(abracé), `rezar` (recé), `gozar` (gocé), `lanzar` (lancé), `analizar` (analicé), `avanzar`
(avancé), `forzar` (forcé).

Spanish avoids writing `ze` and `zi` almost entirely — which is why `z` becomes `c` here, and why
the plural of `lápiz` is `lápices`.

## -ger, -gir, -guir: the mirror image

These have the soft sound in the infinitive, so they change **wherever an `a` or `o` follows** —
the `yo` form of the present, and the whole present subjunctive.

| | coger (-ger) | dirigir (-gir) | seguir (-guir) |
|---|---|---|---|
| presente | co**j**o, coges, coge, cogemos, cogéis, cogen | diri**j**o, diriges, dirige, dirigimos, dirigís, dirigen | si**g**o, sigues, sigue, seguimos, seguís, siguen |
| pres. subj. | co**j**a, co**j**as, co**j**a, co**j**amos, co**j**áis, co**j**an | diri**j**a, diri**j**as, diri**j**a, diri**j**amos, diri**j**áis, diri**j**an | si**g**a, si**g**as, si**g**a, si**g**amos, si**g**áis, si**g**an |
| pretérito | cogí, cogiste, cogió, cogimos, cogisteis, cogieron | dirigí, dirigiste, dirigió… | seguí, seguiste, **siguió**, seguimos, seguisteis, **siguieron** |

**-ger / -gir (g → j):** `coger` (cojo), `escoger` (escojo), `recoger` (recojo), `proteger`
(protejo), `dirigir` (dirijo), `exigir` (exijo), `elegir` (elijo), `corregir` (corrijo), `fingir`
(finjo), `surgir` (surjo).

**-guir (gu → g):** `seguir` (sigo), `conseguir` (consigo), `perseguir` (persigo), `distinguir`
(distingo), `extinguir` (extingo). Here the `u` was never pronounced — it was only there to keep
the `g` hard before `e`/`i`. Before `o` or `a` it has no job, so it goes.

`elegir` and `corregir` do two things at once: the spelling change **and** an `e→i` stem change.
`elijo`, `eliges`, `elige`, `elegimos`, `elegís`, `eligen`.

## -cer and -cir after a consonant (c → z)

| | vencer | convencer | esparcir |
|---|---|---|---|
| presente | ven**z**o, vences, vence, vencemos, vencéis, vencen | conven**z**o, convences… | espar**z**o, esparces… |
| pres. subj. | ven**z**a, ven**z**as, ven**z**a, ven**z**amos, ven**z**áis, ven**z**an | conven**z**a… | espar**z**a… |

Also `ejercer` (ejerzo), `torcer` (tuerzo — with a stem change too).

**Do not confuse these with the `-zco` group.** After a **vowel**, `-cer`/`-cir` verbs take
`-zco` instead: `conocer` → **conozco**, `parecer` → **parezco**, `conducir` → **conduzco**. The
letter before the ending decides which of the two happens.

| | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| yo | ven**z**o, conven**z**o, ejer**z**o | cono**zco**, pare**zco**, condu**zco** |

## -uir: i becomes y

Verbs ending in `-uir` (but not `-guir` or `-quir`) insert a `y` wherever the `i` would otherwise
sit unstressed between vowels.

| | construir | huir | incluir |
|---|---|---|---|
| presente | constru**y**o, constru**y**es, constru**y**e, construimos, construís, constru**y**en | hu**y**o, hu**y**es, hu**y**e, huimos, huís, hu**y**en | inclu**y**o, inclu**y**es, inclu**y**e, incluimos, incluís, inclu**y**en |
| pretérito | construí, construiste, constru**y**ó, construimos, construisteis, constru**y**eron | huí, huiste, hu**y**ó, huimos, huisteis, hu**y**eron | incluí, incluiste, inclu**y**ó, incluimos, incluisteis, inclu**y**eron |
| pres. subj. | constru**y**a, constru**y**as, constru**y**a, constru**y**amos, constru**y**áis, constru**y**an | hu**y**a, hu**y**as, hu**y**a, hu**y**amos, hu**y**áis, hu**y**an | inclu**y**a… |
| gerundio | constru**y**endo | hu**y**endo | inclu**y**endo |

Also `destruir`, `concluir`, `contribuir`, `distribuir`, `influir`, `sustituir`, `atribuir`,
`disminuir`.

## -eer, -aer, -oír: i becomes y in the third person

The same rule, reached from the other direction — the stem already ends in a vowel.

| | leer | creer | caer | oír |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pretérito, él | le**y**ó | cre**y**ó | ca**y**ó | o**y**ó |
| pretérito, ellos | le**y**eron | cre**y**eron | ca**y**eron | o**y**eron |
| gerundio | le**y**endo | cre**y**endo | ca**y**endo | o**y**endo |
| participio | le**í**do | cre**í**do | ca**í**do | o**í**do |
| imp. subj. | le**y**era | cre**y**era | ca**y**era | o**y**era |

The accent on `leído`, `creído`, `caído`, `oído` keeps the `i` as a syllable of its own. Without
it the word would be read as one syllable fewer.

## -iar and -uar: an accent appears

Some `-iar` and `-uar` verbs stress the `i` or `u` in the boot, and then need a written accent to
show it.

| | enviar | continuar | actuar |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | env**í**o | contin**ú**o | act**ú**o |
| tú | env**í**as | contin**ú**as | act**ú**as |
| él / Ud. | env**í**a | contin**ú**a | act**ú**a |
| nosotros | enviamos | continuamos | actuamos |
| vosotros | enviáis | continuáis | actuáis |
| ellos / Uds. | env**í**an | contin**ú**an | act**ú**an |

Also `guiar` (guío), `esquiar` (esquío), `confiar` (confío), `variar` (varío), `criar` (crío),
`graduarse` (me gradúo), `situar` (sitúo), `acentuar` (acentúo).

**Most `-iar` verbs do not do this**, and take no accent: `estudiar` → **estudio**, `cambiar` →
**cambio**, `limpiar` → **limpio**, `copiar` → **copio**, `odiar` → **odio**, `anunciar` →
**anuncio**. As with stem changes, it is a property of the individual verb.

## -guar: the diaeresis

| | averiguar |
|---|---|
| pretérito, yo | averi**gü**é |
| pres. subj. | averi**gü**e, averi**gü**es, averi**gü**e, averi**gü**emos, averi**gü**éis, averi**gü**en |

The two dots keep the `u` pronounced. Without them `gue` would be read as in `guerra`, with a
silent `u`. Also `apaciguar` (apacigüé), `atestiguar` (atestigüé).
