This section is a set of lookup tables. The pages in **Verbs** explain *why* a tense exists and
*when* to reach for it; these pages just show you every form, laid out the same way every time,
so you never have to reconstruct one from a rule.

## How to read these tables

Every table in this section uses the same six columns, in the same order:

| | yo | tú | él / Ud. | nosotros | vosotros | ellos / Uds. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hablar | hablo | hablas | habla | hablamos | habláis | hablan |

- **él / Ud.** covers *él*, *ella* and *usted* — one form for all three.
- **ellos / Uds.** covers *ellos*, *ellas* and *ustedes*.
- **vosotros** is the informal plural of Spain. It does not exist in Latin America. It is
  included here because a reference should be complete — see [tú or usted?](usted) for whether
  you need to produce it.
- Bold marks the part that is doing something unexpected.

## The fourteen tenses

Spanish has eight simple tenses (one word) and, sitting behind each of them, a compound tense
(`haber` + participle) meaning *"having already done it"*. Here is the entire set, in the yo form:

| tense | hablar | comer | vivir | roughly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| presente | hablo | como | vivo | I speak |
| pretérito indefinido | hablé | comí | viví | I spoke |
| pretérito imperfecto | hablaba | comía | vivía | I used to speak |
| futuro simple | hablaré | comeré | viviré | I will speak |
| condicional simple | hablaría | comería | viviría | I would speak |
| presente de subjuntivo | hable | coma | viva | (that) I speak |
| imperfecto de subjuntivo | hablara | comiera | viviera | (that) I spoke |
| pretérito perfecto | he hablado | he comido | he vivido | I have spoken |
| pluscuamperfecto | había hablado | había comido | había vivido | I had spoken |
| futuro perfecto | habré hablado | habré comido | habré vivido | I will have spoken |
| condicional compuesto | habría hablado | habría comido | habría vivido | I would have spoken |
| perfecto de subjuntivo | haya hablado | haya comido | haya vivido | (that) I have spoken |
| pluscuamperfecto de subjuntivo | hubiera hablado | hubiera comido | hubiera vivido | (that) I had spoken |
| imperativo | habla | come | vive | speak! |

That looks like a great deal. It is less than it appears, for three reasons:

1. **The compound half is free.** Once you can conjugate `haber`, all seven compound tenses are
   `haber` + a participle that never changes. Seven tenses, one table — see
   [the compound tenses](conjugacion-compuestos).
2. **Future and conditional share a stem.** Learn twelve irregular stems once and you have
   covered both — see [future and conditional](conjugacion-futuro-condicional).
3. **The imperfect has three irregular verbs in the entire language.**

## Now compare the last two tables

Put the two tables side by side and compare the **endings**. Across eight tenses and six persons
— forty-eight slots — the `-er` and `-ir` families take a different ending in exactly three of
them:

| | comer | vivir |
|---|---|---|
| presente, nosotros | com**emos** | viv**imos** |
| presente, vosotros | com**éis** | viv**ís** |
| imperativo, vosotros | com**ed** | viv**id** |

Everywhere else the endings are identical. The future and conditional look like exceptions —
`comeré` against `viviré` — but they are not: both tenses bolt the ending onto **the whole
infinitive**, so the ending is `-é` in each case and the difference belongs to the infinitive
rather than to the tense.

This is why it is fair to say Spanish has two sets of endings rather than three, and why the
`-ir` column is so often left out of textbook tables.

## Where the irregularity actually lives

Nearly all of it is concentrated in four places. If you know these four, you can conjugate almost
anything:

| what changes | where it shows up | page |
|---|---|---|
| a vowel in the stem (e→ie, o→ue, e→i) | present, and the two tenses built from it | [stem changes](conjugacion-cambios-vocalicos) |
| a letter, to protect a sound | preterite yo, present subjunctive, commands | [spelling changes](conjugacion-cambios-ortograficos) |
| the whole preterite stem | preterite, and the imperfect subjunctive built on it | [preterite](conjugacion-preterito) |
| the future stem | future and conditional | [future & conditional](conjugacion-futuro-condicional) |

The verbs that ignore all four systems and simply do as they please number about twenty. They are
all set out form by form in [the essential ten](verbos-irregulares-esenciales) and
[twelve more](verbos-irregulares-frecuentes).
