Every irregular verb you are likely to meet, in one alphabetical list. Four columns is enough to
pin any Spanish verb down, because everything else follows from them:

| column | why it settles the rest |
|---|---|
| **presente** | the yo form generates the whole present subjunctive and the `usted` commands; the tú form shows whether there is a boot |
| **pretérito** | the `ellos` form of this generates the whole imperfect subjunctive, with no exceptions |
| **elsewhere** | the future stem covers the conditional too; an irregular participle covers all seven compound tenses |
| — | the **imperfect** is not listed, because only `ser`, `ir` and `ver` are irregular in it |

"regular" in the preterite column means the ordinary endings on the ordinary stem — `-é, -aste,
-ó` or `-í, -iste, -ió`. Where two present forms are given, the second shows the stem change.
Bold marks a form you could not have guessed.

## Compounds inherit everything

A prefixed verb behaves exactly like its parent. This is worth more than it sounds — it silently
covers several hundred verbs that never need to be looked up:

| parent | inherited by |
|---|---|
| tener | mantener, obtener, contener, detener, sostener, entretener, retener |
| poner | proponer, componer, suponer, imponer, exponer, disponer, oponer |
| hacer | deshacer, rehacer, satisfacer, contrahacer |
| decir | predecir, contradecir, bendecir, maldecir |
| venir | convenir, prevenir, intervenir, provenir |
| traer | atraer, contraer, distraer, sustraer, extraer |
| ver | prever, entrever |
| volver | devolver, envolver, revolver, resolver |
| seguir | conseguir, perseguir, proseguir |
| escribir | describir, inscribir, prescribir, suscribir |
| poder, salir, caer, oír | (no common compounds worth listing) |

`Mantener` is `tener` with a prefix: **mantengo, mantuve, mantendré, mantén, mantenido**. You
never learned it — you already knew it.

Two small exceptions inside the pattern: `bendecir` and `maldecir` take **regular** participles
(`bendecido`, `maldecido`) and **regular** futures (`bendeciré`), unlike `decir` itself.
